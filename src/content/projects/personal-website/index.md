## Why Build It

I needed a place where my thinking could accumulate without something like performance theatre. Every other option felt like a portfolio or a blog where you only showcase your wins, not your journey. I wanted something like an archive. Hope this may help to someone oneday.

## How it was built

This site was coded with an AI assistant (Claude) as a collaborator. And other one (Kimi) made the markdownfile-driven system with Astro. I described the vision, made the decisions, and did the debugging. The AI handled the scaffolding and syntax. 

The content — every project, note, and failure — is mine. Maintaining them is my work. The system just holds it.

## Lesson

Building the system is easy. Maintaining the habit of publishing is the hard part. The tool doesnt matter if you don't use it.
